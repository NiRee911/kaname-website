Script - not the model

Three agents · own context, read-only

parallel - one message, three calls

ticket-reader

Symptom verbatim, who is affected and who is not, timing, reproduction cases. Never guesses a cause - that is somebody else's column.

flags instructions aimed at automation

code-archeolog

Locates the frames, reads blame, separates change from deploy from restart, checks whether a test existed - and names every other place that shares the suspect code.

blast radius is mandatory

precedent

Same shape in the history, and the one question that matters: was it fixed, or only stopped? A restart is a mitigation, so the defect is still there.

empty commit list = nothing was fixed
Every claim carries one tag
FACThas an artefact - file, line, commit
OBSERVATIONsomebody saw it, no artefact
ASSUMPTIONa working theory, said out loud
CORRELATIONhappened at the same time - not a cause
KNOWN UNKNOWNwe know we don't know; obtainable
TIMELINE GAPno data exists; nobody can obtain it
The last two look alike and are not. One means ask somebody. The other means fix the monitoring.

What comes out · two shapes

analysis.md

Up to three ranked hypotheses. Each one carries its evidence, what stands against it, and the cheapest check that kills it.

kubectl exec <pod> -- python -c \ "from geofence.tz import _offset; print(_offset('Europe/Madrid'))"
1:00:00 confirms it · 2:00:00 kills it. Two minutes, no deploy.
a wrong hypothesis costs two minutes; a wrong answer costs two hours

comment.md

Exactly what would be posted to the ticket - labelled AI-generated, hypotheses not verdict, signal count included.

not posted anywhere in this PoC
production: internal comment only - a support bug is a JSM ticket

questions.md

What the reporter still has to answer, and separately what nobody could have told us. Across dozens of runs this becomes a map of what bug reports systematically miss.
