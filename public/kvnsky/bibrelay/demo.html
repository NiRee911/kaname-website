What this is

routemaster assigns vehicles to delivery routes and computes trip pricing for the LATAM fleet. Node.js service, PostgreSQL, one external maps API. ~210 source files, 6 contributors historically, 2 active.

Where to start reading

1 - dispatch/engine.js (the core loop, and the churn hotspot) · 2 - pricing/ (revenue-critical, single-author) · 3 - api/routes.js (the public surface, stable)
