Or: not a pattern

Three escapes share a label. Three is not a pattern, so it is listed as declined rather than reported. The failure mode of this kind of tool is not missing things - it is confident clusters that are not there.

The leak is a property of the net

Never of a person or a role. No name appears in any artifact, and there is no field to produce one from.

UNKNOWN is not zero

Zero means nothing escaped. Unknown means we cannot see. Merging them is the difference between measuring and theatre.

Eight cases or it is not reported

A pattern below the threshold is named as declined, so nobody thinks the run missed it.

No target invented

Nothing here says 72.7 % is good or bad. A tool that writes its own standard is not measuring anything.
