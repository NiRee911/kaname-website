One record, three rooms

Where this goes, and what is actually running today.

How it works → Watch the demo → ← kvnsky

The same twenty sprints answer three different rooms: the team every two weeks, the ART every quarter, and the business once a year. What changes between them is the zoom, not the evidence.

Each room asks its question today and gets an answer from someone's confidence. The record already contains a better one. Below: what each room would get instead, what it costs to be wrong there, and - stated plainly - which of the three exists right now.

How many of these features fit at 85 % confidence - and which items are historically too large to finish in one sprint, so the split happens before the event instead of eating the first morning of it.

answered today by
Dates, and a buffer someone added at the end because it felt thin.
answered from the record
Buffers sized from measured variability rather than a flat percentage, the estimation bias the team already has (points against actual cycle time), and the seasonality that shows up every year and surprises everyone every year.

Why the order matters · the cost of a wrong answer, by room

One sprintvisible, recoverable, nobody is surprised
One quarterplus every dependency drawn on it
One yearplus the credibility of the people who presented it

The effort to answer well is roughly the same in all three rooms - it is the same record, read at a different zoom. The cost of answering badly is not. That asymmetry is the whole case for doing this at all, and it is also why horizon one is built first: it is the cheapest place to find out the method is wrong.

What holds at every horizon

Never one number

A single figure becomes a commitment within a week and a measuring stick shortly after. Every answer carries its confidence.

The model never computes

Simulation runs in a script with a fixed seed. The model writes the sentences and none of the numbers.

Refusal is an output

Too little history, no forecast - stated as such. A tool that answers every team is a tool whose answers mean nothing.

Nothing about a person

No individual velocity, no ranking, in any artifact. Capacity is a property of a system.

What exists today, without rounding up:
  • Horizon 1 is running. A script with no dependencies and a self-check, verified on two teams - one that gets a forecast and one that is refused. Every number in its output traces to a single JSON file.
  • Horizons 2 and 3 are the shape, not the build. They stand on the same extract, and the order is a data dependency rather than a roadmap preference: the quarter needs the sprint record, the year needs the quarter's patterns.
  • Nothing here has touched a real system. The demo runs on a synthetic fixture whose shape copies a Jira export and whose content is invented.
