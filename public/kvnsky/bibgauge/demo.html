Remediation - MUST first, gaps before partials

GAPDAT-03 · MUSTNo high-risk processing carries a DPIA reference

Why the standard asks: high-risk processing without a DPIA is the exposure a regulator asks about first.

GAPDAT-04 · MUSTData subject requests have no process, owner or SLA

Why the standard asks: the response clock starts on the request, not on the day someone works out who handles it.

GAPOWN-02 · MUSTNo deputy contact recorded

Why the standard asks: a single named contact is a bus factor of one at the level of the catalogue itself.

PARTIALOBS-01 · MUSTTraffic has no alert; saturation pages nobody

Why the standard asks: un-paged degradation is discovered by the customer, not by the team.

PARTIALOBS-02 · MUSTTwo of four alarms have no runbook link

Why the standard asks: an alert without a response procedure moves the outage from detection to guesswork.

PARTIALOWN-04 · MUSTRunbook last reviewed 2024-11-03 - about 21 months

Why the standard asks: a stale runbook is more dangerous than a missing one, because it is trusted.

Read together, the deputy, the runbook links and the stale review are one situation, not three: after the 2025-09 departures the operational knowledge has neither a second holder nor a current written form.
