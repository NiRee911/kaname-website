GAP4.0 pts · OWN-02 · MUST · R-05 severeNo deputy contact recorded

Why the standard asks: a single named contact is a bus factor of one at the level of the catalogue itself.

GAP3.0 pts · DAT-04 · MUST · R-08 majorData subject requests have no process, owner or SLA

Why the standard asks: the response clock starts on the request, not on the day someone works out who handles it.

PARTIAL2.0 pts · DAT-02 · MUST · R-07 severeTwo personal-data fields have no retention period

Why the standard asks: data without a retention period is kept forever by default, which no legal basis supports.

PARTIAL2.0 pts · OBS-01 · MUST · R-01 severeTraffic has no alert; saturation pages nobody

Why the standard asks: un-paged degradation is discovered by the customer, not by the team.

PARTIAL2.0 pts · OBS-02 · MUST · R-03 severeTwo of four alarms have no runbook link

Why the standard asks: an alert without a response procedure moves the outage from detection to guesswork.

PARTIAL2.0 pts · OWN-04 · MUST · R-03 severeRunbook last reviewed 2024-11-03, about 21 months

Why the standard asks: a stale runbook is more dangerous than a missing one, because it is trusted.
