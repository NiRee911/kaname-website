One YAML per domain. Adding a control is a YAML edit - the engine never changes. Three domains authored, eight deliberately left empty: the content of a standard comes from whoever owns it, never from the audit.

Four verdicts

MEETSevidence satisfies the threshold
PARTIALcovered, but not all the way
GAPevidence shows it is not met
NOT ASSESSABLEno source reached it - measures the audit, not the target

exposure.py - the arithmetic

Consequence of the risk × how far the evidence is from the control. Aggregated per area, ranked into weak links.

deterministic - the model never computes
ordinal points, stated openly; euros only when a named person says one

Waivers, applied after

Accepted risk with an approver and an expiry date. The evaluator never sees them.

the agent judges reality - a waiver is a decision
verdict unchanged, remediation list shorter, record intact

Artifacts · English, per run

conformance.md

Every control, its verdict, the place the evidence came from, and any flag.

the full record - including what is waived
an answer to "does it comply", provable line by line

report.md

Leads with the weak-link map per risk area, then remediation ordered by risk carried, blind spots, accepted exposure, delta.

one assessment of one system - packs are not reported on
the team stays, so remediation is the right output

questions.md

What would unlock a NOT ASSESSABLE verdict, and risk the evidence showed that no control covers yet.

proposed controls - never counted as findings
the audit feeds the standard; the standard produces the findings
From the second run on, a delta separates target changed from standard changed - a number that moves because the bar moved is never reported as progress.
Risk first, rule second

Every control names the risk it reduces. That is what makes MUST answerable instead of asserted.

The standard is data

A new control is a YAML edit. The engine has never been touched to add one.

Not assessable is a number

Unreachable evidence is reported as its own category - it measures our reach, not their quality.

Weak links, not folders

Risk areas cut across packs, so the map shows where the system actually bends.
